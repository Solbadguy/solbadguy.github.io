## [Hardware]
- **Workstation:** ThinkPad X1 Carbon (Gen 10)
- **Display:** Dell U2723QE (4K, 60Hz)
- **Input:** Keychron Q1 Pro (Red switches) + Logitech MX Master 3S

## [Software]
- **OS:** Fedora 44 Workstation + KDE Plasma
- **Terminal:** Konsole (Zsh + Starship prompt)
- **Editor:** Neovim (LazyVim configuration)
- **Browser:** Firefox (with uBlock Origin)
- **Communication:** Telegram, Thunderbird

## [Daily CLI Tools]
- **Multiplexer:** `tmux`
- **Finder:** `fzf` + `ripgrep` (`rg`)
- **Navigation:** `zoxide` (`z`)
- **File ops:** `eza` (instead of `ls`), `bat` (instead of `cat`)
- **Network:** `curl`, `mtr`, `nmap`
